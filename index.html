I am a robotics and electrical engineer based in Bandung. My work sits at the intersection of hardware and autonomy — the parts of engineering where a schematic has to become a physical object that survives a real environment.

My focus is ROS 2, autonomous systems, and custom PCB engineering. I have been building roboboats and working with ROS 2 since college, spending most of my time between the EasyEDA canvas and the soldering bench. Every project is a small argument for the same idea: reliable, machine-grade work, from schematic to field deployment.

I take on full-time roles and select consulting engagements in robotics, PCB design, and ROS 2 systems. If you have a problem that needs to leave the bench, I would like to hear about it.
